Hubtype Vertical Agent Kit · a concept for the Aries group

The complex conversation is where WhatsApp automation breaks. Here's how I'd fix it.

A vertical AI agent that holds a messy, multi-intent insurance journey in one thread and hands over to a human with the full story the moment it should. Packaged as self-serve templates that put Hubtype in front of the mid-market: live in 48 hours, not 6-8 weeks.

2 · How I've solved this before

I ran a startup's whole customer operation on WhatsApp.

I founded a cleaning startup and ran its customer ops on WhatsApp: quotes, bookings, reschedules, complaints. Customers never send one tidy request. They stack three in a thread, add a voice note, and change their mind halfway. I learned where a thread breaks, and what the person picking it up needs to know so the customer never repeats themselves.

Vertical agents for the journeys that break bots, sold self-serve.

  • Confidence- and policy-scored handoff with a full context card in the agent workspace.
  • Four vertical templates, published tiers, 48-hour launch: Aries' roadmap, shipped as product. Aries
