The complex conversation is where WhatsApp automation breaks. This is how I'd fix it.

A vertical AI agent that holds a multi-intent insurance journey together end to end, then hands to a human with full context when confidence drops, plus a self-serve kit that takes Hubtype's enterprise engine to the mid-market in 48 hours, not 6-8 weeks.

1 · Where the issues are

Reviewers say Hubtype automates simple queries well but complex issues still fall over to humans, that the service can be slow, and that a 6-8 week team-led launch prices out everyone below enterprise.

2 · How I've solved this before

I founded a cleaning startup and ran its whole customer operation on WhatsApp: quotes, bookings, reschedules, complaints, staff. I know exactly where a conversation thread breaks and what a handoff needs to carry.

3 · How I'd solve it for you

Vertical agents built for the journeys that break bots, with confidence-scored handoff that passes a full context card to the agent workspace. Shipped below as a working demo, then packaged for the mid-market.
