# ELI5

Hubtype sells big companies AI agents that answer customers on WhatsApp. It works well for simple questions, but reviewers say tricky, multi-part problems still get dumped on humans, and only big companies can buy it: no prices on the site, and launching takes a 6-8 week project with Hubtype's team.

This concept shows two fixes as one product:

1. A WhatsApp agent built for the hard conversations, not the easy ones. In the demo, a customer crashes her car, starts a claim, adds breakdown cover mid-conversation, sends a voice note with the other driver's number plate, and then mentions her neck hurts. The agent handles all three jobs in one thread, and when the injury comes up - the thing it should not handle alone - it passes the whole story to a human specialist so she never repeats herself.

2. A self-serve kit for smaller companies: ready-made agents for insurance, airlines, retail and utilities, with published prices, live in 48 hours instead of 6-8 weeks.

That pairing is exactly the plan Aries said it has for Hubtype: industry-specific agents, voice, and a stronger go-to-market.
