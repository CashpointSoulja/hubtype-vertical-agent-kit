# ELI5

Hubtype sells big companies AI agents that answer customers on WhatsApp. Reviewers say it's great at simple questions, but tricky, multi-part problems still end up with a human. Only big companies can buy it: there's no price on the website, and launching takes a 6-8 week project with Hubtype's team.

I built a concept that fixes both:

1. **An agent built for the hard conversations.** In the demo, Sofia crashes her car. She starts a claim, adds breakdown cover halfway through, and sends a voice note with the other driver's number plate. The agent does all three in one chat. Then she says her neck hurts. That's something the agent shouldn't handle alone, so it passes the whole story to a human specialist, Marta, who picks up without asking Sofia to repeat anything. If Sofia never mentions an injury, nobody gets pulled in.

2. **A kit smaller companies can buy themselves.** Ready-made agents for insurance, airlines, retail and utilities, with prices on the page, live in 48 hours instead of 6-8 weeks.

That's the plan Aries announced for Hubtype: industry-specific agents, voice, new markets and a stronger way of selling.
