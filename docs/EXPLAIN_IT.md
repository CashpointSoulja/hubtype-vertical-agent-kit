# Explain-it card

**What:** A WhatsApp AI agent that holds a messy, multi-part customer conversation in one thread (claim, policy change, voice note) and hands over to a human with the full story when it should, sold as self-serve vertical templates.

**How:** Cross-intent state carries entities through the journey; per-turn confidence plus policy rules (like "injury goes to a licensed handler") trigger the handoff; the human gets a structured context card and continues in the same thread.

**Why:** Hubtype's reviewers say complex issues still fall over and service can be slow, and only enterprises can buy it today. This is Aries' stated Hubtype roadmap (verticals, voice, a revamped go-to-market) in one working concept.
